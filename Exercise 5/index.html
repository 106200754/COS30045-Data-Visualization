Things to Consider

Screen Size

Larger televisions may require more electricity depending on the model and technology used.

Usage Time

The amount of time a television is switched on can influence its overall energy consumption.

Energy Rating

Energy ratings can provide useful information when comparing different television models.

Making an Informed Choice

Consumers can consider energy efficiency alongside picture quality, screen size, features and price when selecting a television.
