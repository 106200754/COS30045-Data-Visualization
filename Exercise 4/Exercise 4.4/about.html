Our Purpose

PowerWise is a website designed to provide information about household appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

The website focuses on presenting energy-related information in a simple and accessible format to help users better understand their appliance choices.

Our Focus

One of our areas of focus is television energy consumption. By understanding factors that affect electricity usage, consumers can make more informed decisions about household appliances.
